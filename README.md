## 📖 Certificate Details

### 1. 🏆 Top Performer Recognition – DEPI Initiative
Awarded by the **Ministry of Communications and Information Technology (MCIT)** in recognition of being one of the **top-performing students** in the Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI) across my governorate. This reflects consistent high performance throughout the Microsoft Data Engineer training track.

### 2. Microsoft Data Engineer – Digital Egypt Pioneers Program
A 6-month intensive track (**June – December 2025**) sponsored by MCIT under the Digital Egypt Pioneers Program, delivered in partnership with AMIT, Leading, El-Harefa, and OTO Courses. Covered core data engineering concepts aligned with the Microsoft learning path — data pipelines, storage, processing, and cloud-based data solutions.

### 3. Machine Learning for Data Analysis
Completed under the **Digital Egypt Youth Program (NTI – National Telecommunication Institute)**, from **21/08/2025 to 18/09/2025**. Included **90 technical hours** plus **30 coaching hours** on freelancing, finishing with a score of **92%**. Focused on applying machine learning techniques to real-world data analysis problems.

### 4. Business English Track – Round 3 (DEPI Star Certified)
Completed the **Business English Track (Round 3)** as part of the Digital Egyptian Pioneers program, delivered via OTO Courses between **June and December 2025**. Earned the **DEPI Star** certification, reflecting strong professional English communication skills relevant to technical and business contexts.

### 5. Build with AI – GDG OnCampus
Attended the **"Build with AI"** workshop hosted by **Google Developer Groups (GDG) OnCampus – Arab Open University (2026)**. Gained hands-on experience with **Generative AI**, **Prompt Engineering**, and practical use of Google's AI technologies to solve real-world challenges.

### 6. The Principles of Writing Clean Code
A focused **1-hour 15-minute** course via **Mahara-Tech (ITI Platform)** covering the fundamentals of writing clean, maintainable, and readable code — a core software engineering practice applied across all my projects.

### 7. How to Present the Business Plan and Financial Feasibility
The third and final course in a specialization on business planning via **Edraak**, covering how to present a business plan and financial feasibility study to different types of stakeholders, understand financing tools, and persuade investors professionally. **8 training and interactive hours.**

### 8. AI and Personal Brand
A **4-hour** training session by **The Way Foundation**, completed with an **Excellent** grade, focused on leveraging AI to build and strengthen a personal brand — relevant to professional visibility and career growth in tech.
